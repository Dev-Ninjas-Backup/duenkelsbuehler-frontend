🔒 Core Data Disclosure Principles (Executive Summary)

  • Zero Data Selling & Zero Targeted Advertising: AristoPay does not sell, rent, or trade personal information with data brokers, ad networks, or behavioral retargeting platforms.
  • Strictly Operational Disclosures: User data is disclosed strictly to trusted third-party service processors required to fulfill core escrow marketplace functionality, digital contract signing, identity verification, and subscription billing.

🚀 Crucial Custom Services to Add (Under "Other / + Add More")

Because AristoPay is an Escrow & Legal Contract platform, several core integrations do not appear in generic dropdown menus. These 4 entries must be added using the + Add More button at the bottom of the Termly page:

Purpose Field Service Name Detailed Reason & Data Disclosed
Identity Verification & KYC Veriff • Why: Verifies government-issued ID documents and facial biometric selfies for Service Providers and Clients to satisfy AML/KYC legal standards.
• Data Disclosed: ID documents, biometric photos, legal name, and verification status (src/main/verif/).
Escrow Payment Processing Trustap • Why: Holds milestone funds securely in escrow until deliverables are inspected and approved.
• Data Disclosed: User email, guest user ID, transaction amounts, currency, and milestone release states (src/main/trustap-transaction/).
Digital Contracts & E-Signatures DocuSign • Why: Generates legally binding contracts and collects cryptographic digital signatures between parties.
• Data Disclosed: Signer full names, email addresses, contract terms, digital signature timestamps, and executed contract PDF archives (src/main/docusign_2.0/).
Transactional Email Delivery SMTP / Nodemailer • Why: Delivers critical system transactional notices (OTP verification codes, password resets, deal status alerts, and payment receipts).
• Data Disclosed: User email address, first name, and notification message content (src/common/config/mail/).
